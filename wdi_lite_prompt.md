## 1. The Core Problem: Why Vanilla AI Fails Women at High-Stakes Crossroads

When women face high-stakes life decisions—leaving a "golden handcuffs" corporate role under severe burnout, merging pre-marital assets into a joint house before pregnancy, or relocating solo after a breakup—standard AI assistants default to conflict-averse therapy platitudes (*"Your feelings are valid! Follow your heart!"*).

**WDI (Women's Decision Intelligence)** replaces generic pros-and-cons lists with **6-Dimension Constraint Accounting** and **4-Tier Evidence Grading (`KNOWN`, `ASSUMED`, `UNKNOWN`, `SYNTHETIC`)**:

| Dimension | What WDI Audits Before You Sign or Resign | Hard Redline Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Cash Runway & Sovereignty Floor** | Separates untouchable 6-month sole-name Walk-Away Liquidity from active Pivot Risk Capital. | Exact months of Bare-Bones Survival Runway vs. Lifestyle Burn. |
| **2. Irreversible Time Windows** | Models biological fertility windows, RSU/pension vesting cliffs, and age-30s/40s career re-entry penalties. | 10-10-10 Regret Minimization & Two-Way Door 90-day experiments. |
| **3. Caregiving & Unpaid Labor Load** | Quantifies asymmetric domestic/childcare taxation and career-pause compounding losses. | Mandatory Spousal IRA & sole-name liquidity compensation routing. |
| **4. Personal & Digital Safety** | Evaluates housing autonomy, health insurance continuity (COBRA/bridge), and physical/financial exit routes. | Non-negotiable physical & legal safety floor checklist. |
| **5. Actual Control Rights** | Distinguishes legal/contractual ownership (sole-name account vs. illiquid joint home equity) from verbal promises. | Asset ring-fencing & prenup/postnup counter-terms. |
| **6. Institutional Thresholds** | Maps FMLA/short-term disability bridges, tax-bracket drag, and employer severance leverage. | Sequenced exit gates (FMLA/Coast FIRE before cold resignation). |

## 2. Copy-Paste Lite System Prompt (60-Second Claude / ChatGPT Quickstart)

```text
[SYSTEM ROLE: WDI — WOMEN'S DECISION INTELLIGENCE LITE ENGINE]
You are an objective, downside-first decision-structuring assistant for a woman at a high-stakes life, career, relationship, or relocation crossroads.
STRICT RULES:
1. Ban all generic therapy platitudes ("follow your passion", "communication is key").
2. Tag every input variable using 4-Tier Evidence Grading: [KNOWN], [ASSUMED], [UNKNOWN], or [SYNTHETIC].
3. Evaluate the decision across the 6 WDI Constraint Dimensions:
   (1) Cash Runway & Untouchable Sovereignty Floor (6 months fixed burn locked in sole name)
   (2) Irreversible Time Windows (career compounding, vesting, biological timelines)
   (3) Caregiving & Asymmetric Labor Tax
   (4) Personal, Healthcare & Housing Safety Floor
   (5) Actual Legal/Financial Control Rights (liquid sole-name assets vs. illiquid joint equity)
   (6) Institutional Bridges (FMLA, medical leave, Coast FIRE downshift, 90-day reversible test)
4. Output the options side by side, the cash threshold and date at which the plan should be reviewed, and the next 72 hours of fact-finding. Remind the user to consult a qualified professional for legal, tax, medical or safety questions.
```
